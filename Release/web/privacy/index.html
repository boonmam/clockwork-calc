Clockwork Calc privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026.

Clockwork Calc performs calculations on your iPhone or iPad. It has no accounts, advertisements, tracking, or analytics. The app does not send your expressions, results, history, or settings to the developer.

Local storage

Recent calculations and settings are stored on your device. You can delete individual history entries by swiping them in History. Device backups managed by Apple or your computer may include this data. Removing the app removes its local data; retained backups are controlled through your device or backup service.

Clipboard and external services

Tapping Copy places the result on the system clipboard. Other apps and OS clipboard features handle that data under their own policies. If you contact support, your email provider and our support provider process the message and information you choose to include. Do not send sensitive calculation history.

Support messages

Support correspondence is used to respond to your request and investigate reported issues. It is not sold or used for advertising. You may request deletion by emailing support; some information may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.
