Getting started

Try 1/2+1/3 in Calculator mode. In CAS, try integrate(x^2,x,0,1) or insert a matrix from Examples. Result format applies to your next calculation. Engineering uses powers of ten in multiples of three; SI symbols use scale labels such as k and µ. Copy retains the numeric value.

Calculation limits

CAS uses radians. Some symbolic operations require domain assumptions. “Convergence unresolved” means a required analysis rule is unavailable; it does not mean that an integral diverges. General equation solving may be incomplete. Read About in the app for syntax, supported features, and licenses.
