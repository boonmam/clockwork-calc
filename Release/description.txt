Clockwork Calc brings readable mathematics to iPhone and iPad. Enter an expression and see fractions, powers, matrices, and integrals in textbook notation.

CALCULATE
Work with exact fractions, scientific functions, complex arithmetic, and common unit conversions. Choose Exact, Decimal, Engineering, or Engineering with SI symbols such as k, M, and µ.

EXPLORE ALGEBRA
Use the offline computer algebra system to expand, factor, simplify, differentiate, and solve supported equations. Work with matrices up to 8 × 8, including inverses, determinants, and row reduction.

INTEGRATE
Find symbolic antiderivatives and evaluate definite integrals. Supported improper integrals use separate endpoint limits. Results clearly distinguish divergence from analysis the engine cannot resolve.

KEEP YOUR WORK
Recall recent calculations, copy results, and reuse answers. Dark, light, and system themes are included. Calculations run on your device with no account required.

Scope: symbolic operations can require domain assumptions and equation solving may be incomplete. Improper-integral support covers a bounded elementary class. Currency exchange rates and graphing are not included.
