No sign-in, backend, subscription, or in-app purchase is required. All calculation libraries and math fonts are bundled for offline use.

Try these examples:
1. Calculator: 1/2+1/3 -> 5/6, displayed as a stacked fraction.
2. Calculator: 4700 with Engineering (SI symbols) selected -> 4.7 k.
3. CAS: invert(matrix([1,2],[3,4])) -> the 2 × 2 inverse.
4. CAS: integrate(exp(-x),x,0,Infinity) -> 1. Tap the information label for convergence notes.
5. CAS: integrate(1/x,x,-1,1) -> divergence; opposing infinities are not cancelled.

The Examples menu includes matrix entry and calculus examples. Mode and result format apply to the next calculation. CAS uses radians and symbols rather than unit conversion. Copy/Ans/Use result retain canonical values rather than display-only SI prefixes.

Third-party licenses and privacy information are in About. There is no user tracking or app-operated data collection. Calculations/history/settings stay local, subject to OS-managed backups. The app only writes to the clipboard after an explicit Copy action.

Before submission, the publisher must supply App Review contact name, phone, and email in App Store Connect. These are intentionally not committed to this repository.
